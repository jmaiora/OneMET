About the app

OneMET helps you see how physical activity affects your glucose and plan carbohydrates around your workouts. It reads glucose from Nightscout or Dexcom Share and activity from Apple Health, then shows a prevention-first guide for before and during exercise.

Frequently asked

Which iPhone do I need?

An iPhone running a recent version of iOS. An Apple Watch and a CGM (via Nightscout or Dexcom Share) make the most of the app, but aren't strictly required to try it.

How do I connect my glucose data?

Open the Profile tab → Glucose Source, and enter your Nightscout site or your Dexcom Share account. The connection is read-only.

Is my data private?

Your health data stays on your device and is used only to show your own trends and guidance. It isn't sold or shared with third parties.

I'm a beta tester and can't install the app.

Make sure the free TestFlight app is installed, and that you opened the invitation on the same iPhone using the email linked to your Apple ID. If it still fails, email me the address you used.

OneMET provides illustrative, educational guidance only. It is not a medical device and does not replace advice from your healthcare team. Always agree insulin and carbohydrate decisions with your clinician.
