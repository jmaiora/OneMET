About the app

OneMET helps you see how physical activity affects your glucose and plan carbohydrates around your workouts. It reads glucose from Nightscout, Dexcom Share or LibreLinkUp (or Apple Health), and workouts, activity and METs from Apple Health and your Apple Watch.

  • Summary — your glucose today, with the before/during/after curve of any workout, activity rings and your MET·min over the last week.
  • Workouts — your session history, each with its own glucose curve and an insight on how it affected you.
  • Plan — pick a sport, duration and intensity, and get a prevention-first fuel plan: whether to start now, and a timeline of carbohydrate intakes during the session.
  • Settings — your profile, targets, glucose source, and Help & FAQ.

Available in English and Spanish, in mg/dL or mmol/L.

Frequently asked

Which iPhone do I need?

An iPhone running iOS 16 or later. An Apple Watch and a CGM (via Nightscout, Dexcom Share or LibreLinkUp) make the most of the app, but aren't strictly required to try it.

How do I connect my glucose data?

Choose a source during the first-run setup, or later in Settings → Glucose Source: enter your Nightscout site, your Dexcom Share account or your LibreLinkUp follower account. Connections are read-only, and passwords are kept in the iPhone Keychain. Dexcom Share keeps about 24 h of data and LibreLinkUp about 12 h, so 14-day figures come from Nightscout or Apple Health. Medtronic CareLink isn't supported.

Who is the fuel plan for?

The carbohydrate guidance is designed for people using insulin — type 1 diabetes, and type 2 on insulin, by pump or injections. For anyone else the Plan tab explains why instead of giving a number; the rest of the app works the same.

Can I change how often I take carbs during a session?

Yes. The plan spaces intakes every 45 minutes by default, but you can choose anything from 20 to 45 minutes — whichever you feel most comfortable with. The amount per hour stays the same; a shorter interval just means smaller, more frequent intakes. Set your default in Settings → Profile → Carb Intake Interval, or change it for a single session with the slider on your fuel plan.

My Apple Watch workouts don't appear.

Check that OneMET still has access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → OneMET, with Workouts, Heart Rate and Active Energy turned on. iOS updates occasionally reset these permissions; if the switches are missing entirely, deleting and reinstalling OneMET and granting access again fixes it.

Is my data private?

Your health data stays on your device and is used only to show your own trends and guidance. Glucose is fetched directly from the service you connect; there is no OneMET server, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

I'm a beta tester and can't install the app.

Make sure the free TestFlight app is installed, and that you opened the invitation on the same iPhone using the email linked to your Apple ID. If it still fails, email me the address you used.

OneMET provides illustrative, educational guidance only. It is not a medical device and does not replace advice from your healthcare team. Always agree insulin and carbohydrate decisions with your clinician.

The fuel plan draws on the 2017 Lancet consensus on exercise in type 1 diabetes (Riddell et al.) and EXTOD. The individual thresholds are published; the way this app combines them has not been validated as an algorithm.
